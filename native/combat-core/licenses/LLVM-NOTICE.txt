canonical_math.rs contains a Rust translation of LLVM libc atan2.h,
atan_utils.h and double_double.h, including their binary64 atan lookup table.

Source implementation consulted on 2026-10-09:
https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/blob/main/libc/src/__support/math/atan2.h
https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/blob/main/libc/src/__support/math/atan_utils.h
https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/blob/main/libc/src/__support/FPUtil/double_double.h

The translation uses the non-FMA double-double operations for Wasm and retains
IEEE signed-zero, finite/subnormal/infinite and NaN branches. The game calls it
at the existing manual group angle calculation, preserving source roster order.
