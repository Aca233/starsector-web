## Guardrail

`visualSpawnType` is not a second simulation spawn type. It only selects renderer treatment and visual-family defaults. Likewise, `beamVisualMode`, `hitGlowBrightenDuration`, deterministic Beam contact cadence, launcher smoke, missile engine/trail fields, `missileExplosionVisualSpec`, independent hit-glow state, and impact presentation profiles do not own simulation. Source-backed charge/active/chargedown timing, flux/sec, ammo, burst cadence, missile kinematics, fuse radii and MIRV behavior are now explicitly simulation-owned fields. Missile top-level visual explosion radius/color still must never overwrite gameplay explosion/core/fuse/collision radii. Reaper remains unguided; Heavy Blaster's real simulation spawn contract is source RAY-style; Sabot's MIRV stage and Beam firing lifecycles are authoritative mechanics rather than renderer inference.
